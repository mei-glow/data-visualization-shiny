\begin{abstract}
The global transition toward electric vehicles (EVs) has been one of the most transformative milestone of automative industry in this century. This projects develops an interative dashboard to demonstrate cross-country EV adoption pat-
terns and identify economic, environmental, and infrastructure factors associated with EV market development. The end users would be policymakers, industry analysts and grid operators.
\end{abstract}
