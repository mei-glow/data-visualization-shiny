\section{Project Overview}
\subsection{Introduction}
The global transition toward electric vehicles (EVs) has been one of the most transformative milestone of automative industry in this century. This projects develops an interative dashboard to demonstrate cross-country EV adoption patterns and identify economic, environmental, and infrastructure factors associated with EV market development. The end users would be policymakers, industry analysts and grid operators.

\subsection{Analytical Narrative}
Describe the story your data tells. What are the key questions you are trying to answer? How does the narrative unfold as you dig deeper into the dataset? 
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Adoption Trends.} 
    
    (a) Which countries saw the fastest EV adoption growth from 2010 to 2024?
    
    (b) What factors drive EV growth: charging infrastructure or other socioeconomic conditions?

    (c) When did major EV markets transition from linear to exponential sales growth?

    \item \textbf{Infrastructure \& Demand.} 

    (a) Is charging infrastructure a leading or lagging indicator of EV adoption?

    (b) Which countries have the lowest charger-to-EV availability?
    
    \item \textbf{Market Composition.} 

    How does the BEV–PHEV market share differ across income groups?
    
    \item \textbf{Forecasting.} 

    (a) Which countries are on track to exceed 30\% EV stock share by 2030?

    (c) If the top five EV markets sustain their 2022–2024 growth rates, how much oil demand could be displaced by 2030?

\end{itemize}
